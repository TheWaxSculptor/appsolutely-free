I’m Yishay Stewart-Mitchell, a creator and technology professional from Jamaica, Queens, and the founder of Appsolutely Free.

My background spans IT support, audio production, programming, and custom electronics. Across those fields, I’ve seen how quickly software costs can add up—especially when you’re learning, starting a business, or trying to bring an idea to life.

I created Appsolutely Free to make useful technology easier to find and understand. The goal is simple: help people discover free and open-source alternatives, understand the tradeoffs, and choose tools that fit their needs and budget.

To me, “free” deserves an explanation. A useful recommendation should tell you whether an app has limits, requires paid hosting, or charges for features you might need. That transparency is part of what I want to build here.

Whether you’re a student, artist, developer, small-business owner, or someone tired of another monthly subscription, I want Appsolutely Free to help you keep creating and learning.

Your budget shouldn’t decide whether you get to start.
