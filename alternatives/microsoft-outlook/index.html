Free alternatives to Microsoft Outlook

Microsoft Outlook by Microsoft costs: Included with Microsoft 365. These 3 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

Mozilla Thunderbird

MPL-2.0 ✓ Last checked: 2026-10
Open source $0 to use
WindowsMacLinuxAndroid

A free email, calendar, and contacts client with strong privacy and extensions.

ReplacesMicrosoft Outlook

Pros

  • Unified inbox, calendar, and contacts
  • Add-on ecosystem
  • Works with IMAP, POP, and Exchange

Cons

  • Design feels dated to some users
  • Exchange setup can need extras
Switching: easy
Get Mozilla Thunderbird

Gmail

Free with a Google account ✓ Last checked: 2026-10
$0 to use
WebiPhone / iPadAndroid

Free email with powerful search, spam filtering, and 15 GB of shared storage.

ReplacesMicrosoft Outlook

Pros

  • Excellent spam protection and search
  • Integrates with Google Calendar and Meet
  • Free apps on every device

Cons

  • Ad-supported business model
  • 15 GB storage is shared across Google services
Switching: easy
Get Gmail

Proton Mail

Free tier, paid plans ✓ Last checked: 2026-10
Limited free plan
WebiPhone / iPadAndroidWindowsMacLinux

Private, end-to-end encrypted email based in Switzerland, with open-source apps.

ReplacesMicrosoft Outlook

Pros

  • End-to-end encryption by default
  • No ads or data mining
  • Open-source, audited apps

Cons

  • Free tier has limited storage
  • Some features require a paid plan
Switching: easy
Get Proton Mail
