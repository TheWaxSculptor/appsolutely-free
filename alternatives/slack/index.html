Free alternatives to Slack

Slack by Salesforce costs: Free tier, paid per seat. These 5 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

Element (Matrix)

14kApache-2.0
Open source
WebWindowsMacLinuxiPhone / iPadAndroid

A secure, decentralized chat app built on the open Matrix protocol with end-to-end encryption.

ReplacesSlack

Pros

  • End-to-end encrypted rooms
  • Decentralized and self-hostable
  • Bridges to other chat services

Cons

  • Onboarding is less simple than Slack
  • Performance varies by server
Switching: moderate
