Free alternatives to TeamViewer

TeamViewer by TeamViewer costs: Free for personal use, expensive for business. These 4 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

HopToDesk

Free for personal and commercial use ✓ Last checked: 2026-10
$0 to use
WindowsMacLinuxiPhone / iPadAndroidWeb

A completely free remote desktop tool with end-to-end encryption for Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iOS.

ReplacesTeamViewer

Pros

  • Free for both personal and commercial use with zero subscription fees
  • Simple 9-digit ID connection similar to AnyDesk/TeamViewer
  • File transfer, chat, and multi-monitor switching included

Cons

  • Proprietary network relays
  • Smaller community than RustDesk
Switching: easy
