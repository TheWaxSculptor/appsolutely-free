Free alternatives to Premiere Pro

Premiere Pro by Adobe Inc. costs: Monthly subscription. These 7 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

FilmCraft

8.5k MIT OR Apache-2.0 ✓ Last checked: 2026-10-09
Open source $0 to use
WindowsMacLinuxWeb

Native video editor with Premiere-style panels, trim tools, color grading, and its own built-in codecs.

ReplacesPremiere Pro

Pros

  • Premiere-style workspaces, tools, and keyboard shortcuts
  • Exchanges timelines as Final Cut XML, OpenTimelineIO, EDL, and AAF
  • Color grading panel with live scopes

Cons

  • The project estimates it is about 50 to 60 percent ready for real work
  • No plugin hosting such as VST3, Audio Units, or OpenFX
Switching: moderate
