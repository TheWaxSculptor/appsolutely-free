Free alternatives to Calendly

Calendly by Calendly costs: Free tier, paid per seat. These 4 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

Cal.com

49kAGPL-3.0
Open source

Open scheduling infrastructure to let people book time with you, self-hosted or hosted.

ReplacesCalendly

Pros

  • Calendly-style booking pages
  • Integrates with major calendars
  • Self-hostable

Cons

  • Some features are enterprise-only
  • Self-hosting takes setup
Switching: moderate

Koalendar

Free tier, paid Pro
Free tier
WebiPhone / iPadAndroid

A clean, modern 1-on-1 booking page software with free Google Calendar sync and no meeting limits on the free plan.

ReplacesCalendly

Pros

  • Unlimited appointments on the free tier
  • Clean, responsive booking page that embeds easily into websites
  • Direct Google Calendar & Outlook integration

Cons

  • Group booking and custom branding require Pro plan
  • Basic email reminders only on free plan
Switching: easy
Get Koalendar

TidyCal

Free tier, $29 lifetime Pro
Free tier
Web

A streamlined booking software by AppSumo for scheduling meetings and paid consultations with a generous free plan.

ReplacesCalendly

Pros

  • Zero recurring subscriptions (free tier + $29 lifetime deal for advanced features)
  • Syncs with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Office 365
  • Accepts payments via Stripe and PayPal on paid tier

Cons

  • Free tier allows up to 2 calendar connections
  • Simpler interface with fewer enterprise integrations
Switching: easy
Get TidyCal

Zcal

Free forever
Free
Web

A 100% free meeting scheduling app that adds personalized video introductions and collective meeting polling with zero paywalls.

ReplacesCalendly

Pros

  • Completely free with unlimited booking links and calendars
  • Record short video/GIF intros to personalize your booking page
  • No branding watermarks or hidden upsells

Cons

  • Smaller user base and third-party integrations than Calendly
  • No native SMS notifications
Switching: easy
