Free alternatives to Power BI

Power BI by Microsoft costs: Per-user subscription. These 4 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

Open source Requires hosting/hardware

A simple open-source BI tool that lets anyone ask questions of your data and build dashboards.

  • Easy for non-technical users

Cons
