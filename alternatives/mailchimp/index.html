Free alternatives to Mailchimp

Mailchimp by Intuit costs: Price grows with your list size. These 3 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

listmonk

24k AGPL-3.0 ✓ Last checked: 2026-10
Open source Requires hosting/hardware
Self-HostedWeb

A high-performance self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager.

ReplacesMailchimp
⚠️
Important limitation: Requires user-managed server, Docker, or hosting infrastructure.

Pros

  • Single binary, very fast
  • Templates and segmentation
  • No per-subscriber fees

Cons

  • You supply your own SMTP
  • Fewer design tools than Mailchimp
Switching: moderate
