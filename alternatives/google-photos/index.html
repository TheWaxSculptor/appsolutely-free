Free alternatives to Google Photos

Google Photos by Google costs: Small free tier, then subscription. These 4 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

Nextcloud

Self-HostedWebWindowsMacLinuxiPhone / iPadAndroid

A self-hosted cloud for files, calendar, contacts, and collaboration that you fully own.

ReplacesDropboxGoogle Photos
⚠️
Important limitation: Requires user-managed server, Docker, or hosting infrastructure.

Pros

  • Files, calendar, contacts, and office in one
  • Full ownership of your data
  • Huge app marketplace

Cons

  • Requires a server to run
  • Can feel heavy without tuning
Switching: hard
