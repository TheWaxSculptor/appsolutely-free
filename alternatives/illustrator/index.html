Free alternatives to Adobe Illustrator

Adobe Illustrator by Adobe costs: Monthly or annual subscription. These 7 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

Krita

GPL-3.0
Open source
WindowsMacLinuxAndroid

A professional painting and illustration app built by artists, with fantastic brush engines.

ReplacesPhotoshopAdobe Illustrator

Pros

  • Industry-grade brush engine
  • Animation tools and vector layers
  • Great tablet and pen support

Cons

  • Less suited to photo retouching than GIMP
  • Heavier on large canvases
Switching: easy
Get Krita

Inkscape

GPL-2.0
Open source
WindowsMacLinux

A powerful vector graphics editor for logos, icons, and illustrations using the open SVG format.

ReplacesAdobe Illustrator

Pros

  • Native SVG workflow
  • Bezier, node, and path tools
  • Extensible with plugins

Cons

  • Can slow down on very complex files
  • CMYK workflow is limited
Switching: moderate
Get Inkscape

Photopea

Free with ads
Free
Web

A free Photoshop-style editor that runs entirely in your browser and opens PSD, AI, XD, Sketch, and RAW files.

ReplacesPhotoshopAdobe Illustrator

Pros

  • Looks and feels like Photoshop, so there is almost no learning curve
  • Opens and saves PSD files with layers intact
  • Nothing to install; works on any computer with a browser

Cons

  • Free version shows ads (a paid plan removes them)
  • Heavy files depend on your browser and RAM
Switching: easy
Get Photopea

Affinity by Canva

Free (account required)
Free
WindowsMac

A professional photo, vector, and layout suite that Canva made free to use.

ReplacesPhotoshopAdobe Illustrator

Pros

  • Pro-level raster, vector, and layout tools in one app
  • Imports PSD and AI files
  • No subscription

Cons

  • Some AI features need a paid Canva plan
  • Requires a free Canva account
Switching: moderate
Get Affinity by Canva
