Free alternatives to Wolfram Alpha Pro

Wolfram Alpha Pro by Wolfram Research costs: money. These 3 free apps (many open source) can replace it, with screenshots, pros, and cons.

Dynamic mathematics software that brings together geometry, algebra, spreadsheets, graphing, statistics, and calculus in an intuitive app.

Pros

Cons

  • Non-commercial free license
  • Interface has many modes which can require exploration

Desmos Studio

Free web application
Free
WindowsMacLinuxiPhone / iPadAndroidWeb

A free suite of world-class math tools including the legendary Desmos Graphing Calculator and Scientific Calculator.

ReplacesWolfram Alpha Pro

Pros

  • Instant, responsive graph plotting as you type mathematical functions
  • Sliders, tables of data, animations, and regression curve fitting
  • Available completely free on the web, iOS, and Android with zero ads

Cons

  • Specialized in graphing and algebra rather than general computational trivia queries
  • Step-by-step calculus proofs are limited compared to Wolfram Pro
Switching: easy
Get Desmos Studio

Open source

A high-precision scientific desktop calculator with auto-completion, historical scrollback, and over 80 built-in math functions.

ReplacesWolfram Alpha Pro

Pros

  • Up to 50 decimal digits of precision
  • Instant calculation as you type with autocompletion of variables and constants
  • Keyboard-centric workflow makes quick calculations effortless

Cons

  • Desktop only; no mobile apps
  • Focused on arithmetic and scientific computation, not symbolic CAS solving
