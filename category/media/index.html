Jellyfin

58kGPL-2.0
Open source
Self-HostedWebWindowsMacLinuxiPhone / iPadAndroid

A free software media system to stream your own movies, shows, and music with no tracking.

ReplacesPlex

Pros

  • Completely free, no premium tier
  • No tracking or accounts
  • Clients for almost every device

Cons

  • You host and manage the server
  • Some client apps are less polished than Plex

Spotube

50kBSD-4-Clause
Open source
WindowsMacLinuxiPhone / iPadAndroidWeb

An open-source Spotify client that uses Spotify’s metadata with YouTube as the audio source, giving you ad-free music.

ReplacesSpotify Premium

Pros

  • No Spotify Premium subscription needed
  • No ads and no telemetry
  • Download tracks for offline listening

Cons

  • Relies on YouTube audio matching, so very niche live tracks may mismatch
  • Cannot play Spotify Podcasts
Switching: easy
Source code Get Spotube

Libby by OverDrive

Free (Library Service)
Free
iPhone / iPadAndroidWeb

Borrow thousands of audiobooks and eBooks completely free using your local public library card with no monthly subscription fees.

ReplacesAudible

Pros

  • 100% free legal access to thousands of bestselling audiobooks and eBooks
  • Clean, delightful mobile player with sleep timer, bookmarks, and variable speed

Audiobookshelf

GPL-3.0
Open source
WindowsMacLinuxiPhone / iPadAndroidWeb

A self-hosted open-source audiobook and podcast server with dedicated mobile apps for streaming and multi-device progress sync.

ReplacesAudible

Pros

  • Stream your own audiobook collection anywhere with synchronized playback across devices
  • Fetches audiobook metadata and chapter covers automatically from open databases
  • Free, polished native iOS and Android apps

Cons

  • Requires self-hosting the server via Docker or desktop binary
  • You provide your own audiobook audio files (MP3/M4B)
Switching: moderate
Source code Get Audiobookshelf

LibriVox

Public Domain
Free
WindowsMacLinuxiPhone / iPadAndroidWeb

A non-profit community of volunteers who record and release free public-domain audiobooks from classic literature.

ReplacesAudible

Pros

  • Completely free access to over 18,000 public domain audiobooks
  • Classic literature from Shakespeare and Austen to Doyle and Verne
  • Download files as DRM-free MP3s with zero registration

Cons

  • Volunteer narrator quality varies across titles
  • Covers historic public domain books only (no contemporary copyrighted books)
Switching: easy
