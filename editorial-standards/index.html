Editorial Standards & Verification Policy

Every app, course, asset, and guide on Appsolutely Free is hand-reviewed with clear criteria. Here is how we verify recommendations and handle corrections.

1. Transparent Pricing Taxonomy

To us, "free" is not a marketing buzzword. Every listing is explicitly classified under one of three transparent tiers:

  • ✨ $0 to use: Free for personal and commercial use with zero recurring software fees (e.g. Blender, GIMP, OBS Studio, Audacity).
  • 🎁 Limited free plan: Free tier exists but has real limits on users, storage, export resolution, or advanced features (e.g. Notion, Figma, Airtable).
  • 🖥️ Requires hosting/hardware: The software license is free and open-source, but running it requires a VPS, cloud server, or home lab hardware (e.g. Penpot, Coolify, Supabase).

2. Zero Bloatware & Privacy First

We reject programs bundled with adware, unwanted browser toolbars, hidden crypto miners, or predatory trackers. Software that degrades user privacy or requires intrusive third-party installers is permanently excluded.

3. Active Maintenance & Health Checks

Open-source repositories are monitored for development velocity, recent commit timestamps, star counts, and open security issues. Abandoned or vulnerable packages (such as outdated OpenOffice builds) carry explicit, prominent warning banners.

4. Commercial Disclosure & Independence

Editorial recommendations are never sold. Listings cannot pay for higher ranking or positive reviews. When optional paid hosting or commercial services are linked (such as VPS providers for self-hosting), they are strictly segregated and transparently marked as Optional Paid Services or Sponsored.

5. Fast Correction Reporting

Software evolves quickly. Every listing card includes a Report outdated information button. Submissions are delivered directly to our verification team and resolved promptly. You can also reach our team at support@appsolutelyfree.com.
